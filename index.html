Professional Scope Notice

Please note: I am not a medical doctor, physical therapist, or chiropractor, and I do not diagnose or treat acute medical conditions. My role is to design safe, progressive exercises that support recovery, posture adjustment, and physical confidence once you are cleared for general exercise.

Post-Rehabilitation Transition

Physical Therapy Over? Bridge the Post-Rehab Gap.

Many patients leave clinical physical therapy with improved baseline function, but struggle to complete their Home Exercise Programs (HEPs) or transition safely into full independent strength. I pick up exactly where your physical therapist left off. By reinforcing your HEP, supervising movement quality, and managing loads safely, I ensure a seamless bridge back to full vitality and a reduction of recurrence of injuries or falls.

HEP Completion Support: Bring structure and accountability directly to your home.
Scientific Progression: Systematically increase chronic loads (ACWR) to prevent pain flare-ups.
Clinical Feedback Loop: Transparent communication and updates sent back to your referring therapist.

Selected Evidence & Rationale

The Post-Rehab Gap & Why Follow-Through Matters

Insurance limits and clinical discharge criteria often leave patients on their own before they are ready for a general gym. Unsupervised training risks re-injury and compensating patterns.

Up to 40%

Quadriceps Deficit

Strength deficits can persist in the quadriceps long after formal physical therapy discharge, even when baseline pain has improved.

Source: Mizner et al. 2005
Up to 51%

Slower Stair-Climbing

Functional performance deficits in critical tasks like climbing stairs or carrying loads can remain far beyond the active insurance window.

Source: Clinical Outcome Data
70% - 75%

HEP Non-Adherence

Approximately 3 out of 4 patients fail to consistently follow through on their prescribed Home Exercise Programs without support and accountability.

Source: Adherence Literature

Why Home Exercise Programs (HEPs) Break Down:

  • ✗ No professional accountability between clinic visits.
  • ✗ No progression guardrails or load dosage control.
  • ✗ Fear of pain or intimidation on a commercial gym floor.
  • ✗ Good exercises performed inconsistently do not protect outcomes.
Our Protocol

The Grayson Private Training Solution

An evidence-based transition that applies structured progression to return clients to full movement confidence.

1

HEP Completion & Reinforcement

We begin by reinforcing the specific home exercise plan prescribed by your physical therapist. We focus heavily on movement quality, tolerance, consistency, and proper activation before adding resistance.

2

Phased Progression

Once the initial HEP is comfortable and established, we transition into loaded progressions. We target everyday functional strength, joint stabilization, balance, bone density support, and fall prevention.

3

Structured Load Management (ACWR)

To avoid joint flares, workload progressions are strictly managed. We track chronic workload weekly. We maintain the Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) in the target zones: 0.80 to 1.30 (with weekly increases kept at ≤ 10%).

4

Individualized Programming & Aging at Home

Every program is customized to support joint preservation, gait symmetry, and confidence. All assessment gear and loading tools are brought directly to the client's home in Ann Arbor and surrounding areas.
